FAQ

Before your first visit.

Can't find your question? Call us. A person answers, usually on the second ring.

Book a visit Call us
Do I need a referral?

No. You can book a family doctor, a check-up, tests or most specialists directly. If a specialist does need a referral, your family doctor will arrange it.

Do you see children?

Yes, from newborns. Our family doctors see the whole family, and a pediatrician is here on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Do you work with insurance?

We are a self-pay clinic. After each visit you get an itemized receipt, which many insurers accept for reimbursement. Check with your insurer before you book.

How quickly do test results come back?

Most blood tests are back within 48 hours. A few specialized tests take up to a week. Your doctor calls or messages you to explain them.

What is IV therapy at Averin?

Fluids or vitamins given through a drip in our treatment lounge. A doctor assesses you first and only recommends it when there is a medical reason, such as dehydration or a confirmed deficiency.

What can be handled in a video visit?

Follow-ups, test results, repeat prescriptions, minor skin problems and general questions. If your doctor needs to examine you, they will book you in person.

How do I cancel or move a visit?

Call or email us. It's free up to 24 hours before the visit. After that we charge half the visit fee.
